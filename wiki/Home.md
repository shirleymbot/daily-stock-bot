## About

Daily Stock Bot is an automated stock market briefing system that delivers:
- Real-time stock prices
- News analysis with sentiment
- Price alerts
- On-demand Telegram commands

## Features

- ✅ Automated daily reports at 8 AM
- ✅ On-demand Telegram commands (`/price`, `/news`, `/alert`)
- ✅ 24/7 operation with systemd services
- ✅ News sentiment analysis
- ✅ Dynamic price targets
